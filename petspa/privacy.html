Privacy Policy

Paws & Bubbles · Last updated November 13, 2026

Paws & Bubbles is a single-player game made by Jacob Fournier. Your shop, progress and settings are saved only on your device. We don't ask for your name, email or any account, and we don't collect personal information ourselves.

Ads

The game shows ads provided by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and its partner networks. To choose and measure ads, these services may collect device information such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when asked), IP address, device model and operating system, and how you interact with ads. See the Unity privacy policy.

You can say no to tracking when the game asks, or change it later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Ads still appear, but they won't be personalised.

Measuring our own ads (TikTok)

We may advertise Paws & Bubbles on TikTok. To see whether those ads work, the game includes the TikTok Business SDK (by TikTok), which sends TikTok a few app events: the app being installed and opened, the tutorial being finished, new shop reputation levels reached, and the Remove Ads purchase. Along with these events it may send device identifiers (the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking), device model and operating system, app version, network information, and your IP address. It doesn't send your name, email or payment details.

TikTok's handling of this data is described in the TikTok Business Products Terms and the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
