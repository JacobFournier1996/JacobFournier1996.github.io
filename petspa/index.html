Common questions

I bought Remove Ads on another phone, or reinstalled the game.
Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Will I lose my shop if I delete the app?
Yes. Progress is saved only on your phone. Purchases can be restored.
